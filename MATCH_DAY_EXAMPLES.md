## 🚨 Example 6: Avoiding Bad Bets

### Red Flag Example:
```markdown
Match: Everton vs West Ham
Bookmaker Odds: 2.60 (Everton)
Your Analysis: 40% Everton win

Kelly Says: NEGATIVE VALUE (-4%)
Action: NO BET ❌

Why: No edge, likely to lose long-term
```

### Emotional Betting Trap:
```markdown
Scenario: Lost £100 on early match
Temptation: Double next bet to recover

Correct Action:
1. Stick to Kelly stakes
2. Take 10-minute break
3. Review next bet objectively
4. Bet normal amount (not doubled)
```
