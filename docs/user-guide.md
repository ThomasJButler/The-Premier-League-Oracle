## Initial Setup

### Getting Your API Key

1. Visit [Football-Data.org](https://www.football-data.org/client/register)
2. Sign up for a free account
3. Copy your API key from the dashboard
4. Return to Premier League Oracle

### First-Time Setup Wizard

When you first open the app, you'll see our setup wizard:

1. **Welcome Screen** - Introduction to the platform
2. **Privacy & Security** - Learn how we protect your data
3. **API Configuration** - Enter your Football-Data.org API key
4. **Validation** - The app tests your key and confirms it works

After successful setup, the app will automatically reload with live Premier League data.

## Understanding Predictions

Our predictions use multiple statistical models:

### 🎲 Prediction Models

#### ELO Rating System
- **What it is**: Dynamic team strength ratings
- **How it works**: Teams gain/lose points based on results
- **Best for**: Overall team strength comparison

#### Poisson Distribution
- **What it is**: Goal probability modeling
- **How it works**: Uses average goals to predict scores
- **Best for**: Exact score predictions

#### Expected Goals (xG)
- **What it is**: Shot quality analysis
- **How it works**: Evaluates chance creation quality
- **Best for**: Understanding true performance

### Reading Predictions

Each prediction shows:
- **Win/Draw/Loss percentages** - Probability of each outcome
- **Confidence level** - How certain the model is
- **Expected score** - Most likely scoreline
- **Value indicators** - Potential betting value

## Tips for Success

### 🎯 Best Practices

1. **Check Multiple Models**
   - Don't rely on a single prediction
   - Look for consensus between models
   - Understand each model's strengths

2. **Consider Context**
   - Check for injuries and suspensions
   - Review recent form
   - Consider home/away performance
   - Look at head-to-head history

3. **Use the Kelly Calculator**
   - Never bet more than recommended
   - Adjust for your risk tolerance
   - Track your results over time

4. **Monitor Team Trends**
   - Watch for improving/declining form
   - Note manager changes
   - Track player availability

### ⚠️ Common Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Overconfidence**: Even 90% probability means 1 in 10 losses
- **Chasing losses**: Stick to your strategy
- **Ignoring bankroll management**: Use proper stake sizing
- **Emotional betting**: Let data guide decisions
